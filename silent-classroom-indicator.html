PROJECT 008 / EMBEDDED SYSTEMS / INTERACTIVE PRESENTATION

Silent Classroom Indicator.

An Arduino-based classroom noise monitoring concept. The system observes sound levels and activates a visual and audible warning when excessive noise continues for five minutes. Explore the animated waveform, trigger logic, indicator outputs, and automatic alert shutdown.

Press Start Presentation or Test Buzzer Sound once to allow your browser to play the generated buzzer tone.
