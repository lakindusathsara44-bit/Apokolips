The warning timer reaches five minutes before the red alert and buzzer activate. Once active, the alert has its own five-minute automatic shutdown timer. A short sound dip does not immediately erase the trigger timer.

TRIGGER LOGIC

When the input reaches the warning threshold, the yellow warning state is enabled and the high-noise timer begins. The timer resets only after the input remains below the release threshold for 1.5 seconds.

SIMULATION OUTPUT

This page models the system behaviour in the browser. The on-screen indicators and generated buzzer sound are simulation outputs; physical LEDs and a buzzer require the corresponding hardware circuit.
