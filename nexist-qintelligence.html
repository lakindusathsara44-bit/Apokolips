NEXIST Q-Intelligence.

A hybrid research prototype that combines NEXIST evidence-aware lifecycle rules, an open-weight AI planning advisor, and a small QAOA-style qubit state-vector simulator. AI proposes. NEXIST checks evidence. Quantum simulation explores candidate combinations. A classical validator and human decision remain in control.

03 / Quantum selection engine

The p=1 QAOA-style simulator encodes candidate benefit and constraints in a diagonal phase operator, applies an RX mixer, then samples a probability distribution. A small exhaustive classical search is reported beside the result as a reference.

Add candidates and record evidence before running the simulator.

05 / Research limits

This project is a hybrid prototype, not a verified new data structure or a quantum-advantage demonstration.

AI boundary

AI output is structured proposal data. It cannot establish evidence, approve a project, or change NEXIST lifecycle state.

Quantum boundary

The browser and Python engines simulate qubits on a classical machine. The small state-vector circuit is exponential in candidate count and deliberately capped.

Evidence boundary

Source labels and timestamps do not authenticate documents or identities. Real deployment needs access control, signed provenance, and durable audit storage.

Decision boundary

Optimization only returns a recommendation. It never calls realization, spends money, launches infrastructure, or executes real-world actions.

This demonstration stores sample records in this browser's local storage. It is separate from the persistent Python core engine.
