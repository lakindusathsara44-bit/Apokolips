Imaging modes

Three ways to inspect captured data.

  • 01IR Image Capture
    Monochrome intensity map from an infrared imaging sensor.
  • 02Dual-Color Image
    Map intensity into a two-colour blue-to-red visualization.
  • 03Dual-Spectrum Capture
    Compare visible-light and infrared frames from compatible sensors.

Hardware interface

Browser serial requires desktop Chrome or Edge over HTTPS and a compatible serial device.

An IR obstacle sensor only detects proximity; it cannot capture an image. Real imaging requires an IR camera module. Dual-spectrum mode additionally needs a visible-light camera channel.
