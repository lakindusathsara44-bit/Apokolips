Choose your plan.

Simple monthly pricing per subdomain. Choose a standard subdomain or add www support for your website address.

Who can benefit from an Apokolips subdomain?

An Apokolips.xyz subdomain can give your website or project a clear, memorable web address. It may be useful for many different purposes, including:

University student projects Small shops & businesses Personal websites & portfolios Education websites Class or course websites Community projects Blogs & information sites Many other ideas

Have another idea? You are welcome to apply too. Tell us what you want to build and how you plan to use your subdomain.

How to apply

Choose the plan that best matches your needs and select Choose Plan. Your email app will open with an application message prepared for the Apokolips team. Fill in your name, contact number, email address, and a short description of your intended website or project, then send the request. We will review your appeal and contact you about the next steps.

Important: Selecting a plan sends an application request; it does not automatically activate a subdomain or complete a payment.

Choose your own subdomain name

You can request any name you like, subject to availability. Here are a few examples of how your website address could look:

yourname.apokolips.xyz myportfolio.apokolips.xyz mybusiness.apokolips.xyz shop.apokolips.xyz blog.apokolips.xyz project.apokolips.xyz
Please note: These prices are per subdomain per month. The LKR 200 option includes a request to configure both the standard subdomain and its www version; it requires DNS and hosting support for both hostnames. These plans provide subdomains only. Website hosting, storage, and website files are not included. You must have your own hosting provider that supports custom domains. DNS connection and HTTPS availability depend on your hosting provider.
